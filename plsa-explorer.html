An outside-in target-state design for the Portable Long Service Authority (PLSA) Victoria, administering portable long service benefits under the Long Service Benefits Portability Act 2018 (Vic) across three schemes — Community Services, Contract Cleaning & Security. The modelling crux: one worker can accrue across multiple schemes and multiple concurrent employers without cross-contaminating the separate statutory funds — resolved with a single Party identity plus dated associative entities.

The Authority's statutory duty under the Long Service Benefits Portability Act 2018 is to let workers in transient industries accrue long service benefits across many employers within a covered industry, rather than needing seven unbroken years with one. The target state expresses that duty as a set of regulated services over a single authoritative worker and employer identity, with the scheme-specific accrual, levy and claim mechanics carried on dated associative entities so the three statutory funds never blend.

The crux: one worker, many schemes, many employers

The whole design turns on separating identity from participation:

  • A Worker and an Employer are each a single identity record — one natural person, one legal entity — no matter how many schemes or relationships they have.
  • A Worker Scheme Membership links a worker to one scheme; the worker's accrual, threshold and claim all live here, so Community Services service can never be blended with Security service to reach seven years.
  • An Employer Scheme Registration links an employer to one scheme; it dictates which quarterly returns that employer must file.
  • An Employment Engagement ties a worker's scheme membership to an employer's scheme registration over a dated period — this is where concurrent employers and job changes are recorded, always within a single scheme.

Design principles carried into the target state
  • Single source of truth for identity. One Worker (natural person) and one Employer (legal entity), with deduplication as a first-class capability — because a duplicate worker silently splits continuous service and can destroy an entitlement.
  • Scheme funds never blend. Accrual, the 7-year threshold and claims are all computed per scheme membership; the three statutory funds are financially and mathematically independent.
  • Point-in-time accuracy. Engagements and service periods are dated so continuous-service and 4-year-inactivity tests can be evaluated at any historical instant.
  • High-volume by design. A single quarterly return can carry thousands of worker line items; the granular transactional entities are modelled to bulk-load, not to sit on standard assessment objects.
  • Provisional until paid. Reported service is credited provisionally and only confirmed once the employer's levy is reconciled — the levy funds the future liability.
  • Portability across borders. Interstate reciprocal recognition is an explicit integration, not a manual re-key, so a worker's cross-border service can count toward a Victorian claim.

Worked example — one worker, two schemes, two concurrent employers

Priya: Community Services + Security, at the same time

Priya works weekdays for a family-violence support service (Community Services) and weekends for an events security firm (Security). One of her Community Services roles changes employer mid-year. This shows how a single identity fans out through the junctions so the two funds accrue independently and continuous service survives the job change.

Why it holds together: Priya is one Worker. She has two Worker Scheme Memberships — one per fund — and each accrues on its own toward its own 7-year threshold; her Community Services weeks can never top up her Security balance. Her mid-year change of Community Services employer is just a new Employment Engagement under the same membership, so her continuous service in that industry is unbroken. Each employer files its own quarterly return against its own Employer Scheme Registration, and every worker line becomes a Service Period tied to the correct engagement — so the money and the days always land in the right fund.
