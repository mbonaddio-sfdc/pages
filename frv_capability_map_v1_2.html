Core / Mission Capabilities
C1 Incident Response & Operations
Fire Suppression & Mitigation
Contain and extinguish structural, non-structural, bushfire and specialised fires (incl. BESS/lithium-ion events).
Rescue Operations Management
Technical rescue — road crash, rail/aircraft, industrial entrapment, trench, high-angle, confined space.
Hazardous Materials (HAZMAT) Management
Identify, contain, neutralise CBRN threats; mass decontamination; statewide specialist resource.
Emergency Medical Response (EMR)
Rapid life-saving intervention in support of Ambulance Victoria (e.g. priority-zero cardiac arrests).
Marine Response & Port Security
Fire/hazard response in maritime environments; coxswains & vessels for the Port of Melbourne.
C2 Community Fire Safety & Resilience
Community Risk Assessment & Education
Analyse local vulnerabilities, build risk profiles, deliver public education and warnings.
Built-Environment Fire Safety Regulation
Assess/consent fire-engineering designs; Building Regulations 2018 powers; performed for CFA via service deed.
Commercial Fire Services Management
Operate a commercial arm — equipment maintenance, ESM auditing, training (Fire Equipment Services).
Fire Investigation & Root-Cause Analysis
Forensically determine fire origin/cause; support Victoria Police Arson Squad; inform policy.
C3 Incident Command & Coordination
Incident Control & Command
Establish and scale command structures at incidents; operate under AIIMS; scale to ICCs for Class 1 events.
Inter-Agency Interoperability & Coordination
Integrate with VicPol, Ambulance Victoria, VICSES, EMC under the State Emergency Management Plan.
Dispatch & Communications Integration
Interface with statewide dispatch to mobilise resources & manage fireground comms (Triple Zero Victoria CAD).
Enabling / Supporting Capabilities
E1 Workforce & Firefighter Management
Talent Acquisition & Deployment
Recruit, roster and deploy a specialised career workforce; unify legacy MFB & CFA personnel.
Training & Professional Development
Certify and uplift operational and corporate skills; manage qualification matrices.
Firefighter Health & Wellbeing Management
Monitor and protect physical/psychological health; manage cumulative trauma; presumptive-rights claims.
CFA Secondment & Support Management
Administer and support career staff seconded to the CFA; navigate the operating boundary.
E2 Asset, Fleet & Station Management
Fleet & Appliance Management
Procure, maintain and lifecycle-manage emergency vehicles — pumpers, aerials, marine vessels.
Property & Station Management
Acquire, build, maintain and upgrade stations, training campuses and offices (incl. PFAS remediation).
Operational Equipment & PPE Management
Manage inventory, testing and lifecycle of frontline gear & protective clothing to AS/NZS standards.
E3 Information & Digital Technology
Information Security & Cyber Resilience
Protect data, networks and comms infrastructure from disruption and attack.
Incident Reporting & Analytics
Capture, validate and analyse post-incident data; feed statutory/national datasets (AIRS).
Enterprise Architecture & Integration
Bridge operational technology (OT) with corporate IT; ensure interoperable, reliable data flow.
E4 Financial & Corporate Services
Financial Planning & Budget Management
Manage funding streams, capital budgeting and sustainability (Fire Services Property Levy).
Procurement & Contract Management
Source goods/services; manage vendors and probity for major capital acquisitions.
Commercial Revenue & Billing Management
Administer cost recovery and commercial invoicing (false alarms, HAZMAT clean-ups, FES).
Governance / Strategic Capabilities
G1 Strategic Planning & Performance
Enterprise Strategy & Operating-Model Design
Define long-term vision and structure; adapt to climate change and urban densification.
Fire District Boundary & Capability Review
Assess geographic risk; support the statutory 3-member Fire District Review Panel.
Performance Monitoring & Reporting
Measure and report outcomes against statutory obligations; feed the Fire Services Implementation Monitor.
G2 Compliance, Risk & Integrity
Enterprise Risk & Business Continuity
Identify, evaluate and mitigate strategic/operational/financial risk; sustain service during disruption.
Integrity, Conduct & Ethical Standards
Embed a pro-integrity culture; investigate misconduct; govern secure information sharing.
Statutory Compliance & Regulatory Assurance
Operate within OHS, environmental and privacy law; assure regulatory obligations.
G3 Stakeholder & Intergovernmental Relations
Government & Ministerial Relations
Engage the Minister and DJCS; provide policy advice; secure capital funding.
Industrial Relations & Union Engagement
Negotiate EBAs and consult with industrial bodies (UFU) within public-sector conventions.
Inter-Agency Agreement Management
Draft and govern service deeds and MoUs — notably the complex CFA boundary arrangements.
