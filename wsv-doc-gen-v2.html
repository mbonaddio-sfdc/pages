Re: Liability decision — Claim V123987

Dear Mr Smith,

We write regarding your claim for compensation for a lower back injury lodged on 10 January 2026.

After careful review of the medical evidence provided by Dr A. Jones, and having considered the MRI findings dated 8 January 2026, we regret to advise that liability for your claim is rejected.

Under Section 98 of the Workplace Injury Rehabilitation and Compensation Act 2013, an insurer may only accept liability where the injury arose out of or in the course of employment and the employment was a significant contributing factor to the injury. Based on the evidence before us, we are not satisfied that this test is met.

Specifically, the medical evidence indicates that the injury pre-dates the period of employment, and there is insufficient evidence that work duties materially contributed to the current incapacity.

You have the right to request a review of this decision. To do so, please contact the Accident Compensation Conciliation Service within 60 days of the date of this letter.

Yours sincerely,


Jane Doe
Senior Claims Manager
WorkSafe Victoria
